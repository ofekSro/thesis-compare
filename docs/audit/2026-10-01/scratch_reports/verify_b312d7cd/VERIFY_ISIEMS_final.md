## MISMATCH

- **#14** p.2: "agree with Kingery and Bulmash to within approximately 10% in peak overpressure [18]". The study's own reference runs lie −7% to −9% from KB at Z 2–8, −14% (median) at Z 8–12 and −26% at Z 12–20 (`cfd_vs_kb_ufc215.csv`). The sentence cites [18], but it contradicts the repo's own data beyond Z≈8.
- **#44** p.7: "depends mainly on the plan-area density ρ and on s̃". ρ is supported in both dets. In det1, s̃ is the weakest of the three parameters (ΔR²: ρ 0.280, H/s 0.193, s̃ 0.072); in det2 the three are comparable (0.321 / 0.291 / 0.292).
- **#50** p.8: "by two percentage points … by five, in both detonation scenarios". det1 gives +1.75 and +4.85, det2 gives +2.53 and +6.60, pooled +2.14 and +5.73. "Two"/"five" hold for det1 only. A wording that fits both scenarios: "by about two to three percentage points … and by five to seven".
- **#56** p.8: "was fixed in advance and not fitted". The values (1, 2) are not least-squares parameters, but `convergence_models.py:40` records a as "selected by CV from {1, sqrt(2), 2}". "In advance" is contradicted. A possible wording: "was selected from {1, √2, 2} by cross-validation and then held fixed, not fitted".
